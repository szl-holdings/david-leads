# David Leads - David's operating guide

David Leads is a public-record research workspace built for David Abraham's
published focus on life insurance, long-term care, annuities, and protection
planning. It helps answer one practical question:

> Which organization changed, why might the timing matter, and what should I
> verify next?

It is not a purchased contact list and it does not create permission to call.

## Open the application

Go to:

https://szlholdings-david-leads.hf.space/

The research view opens without a username, password, or access key. Private
broker work, saved notes, contact channels, clearance decisions, and exports
remain protected and are never shown in the public view.

## Start your day

1. Read the **Broker brief** near the top. It summarizes the strongest timing
   pattern in the current official-source pull.
2. Choose **All East**, a region, or one state. Selecting one state runs a
   deeper state-specific search.
3. Use the deal-moment buttons:
   - **Life-plan timing** shows organizations whose official Form 5500 filing
     reports a group-life benefit and a plan or policy anniversary.
   - **Growth & awards** shows current federal contract activity.
   - **Operational change** shows carrier registrations and facility activity.
   - **Needs research** shows everything that still requires verification.
4. Open an account to see its timing, likely fit, evidence, limitations, and
   three-step broker action.

## Read each account correctly

- **Verified deal moment** is the fact returned by the official source.
- **Timing** is a research window. A Form 5500 anniversary is not proof of a
  renewal, dissatisfaction, or a buying decision.
- **Likely fit** is a reason to investigate. It is not a recommendation,
  eligibility decision, quote, or underwriting conclusion.
- **Evidence** tells you whether the item comes from a direct filing or another
  official record and whether a source receipt is attached.
- **Cleared to contact** stays at zero in the public view. That is intentional.

## The three-step broker action

1. **Verify the moment.** Open the official source and confirm the organization,
   reported date, and relevant filing field.
2. **Qualify the fit.** Use the organization’s own website to understand its
   current operations and find a business-published channel.
3. **Clear outreach.** In the protected operator workflow, complete
   suppression, licensing, state-rule, purpose, and channel checks before any
   call or email.

## What the application never assumes

David Leads never treats public visibility as consent. It does not infer
health, age, race, religion, family status, wealth, insurability, or private
contact details. It does not scrape social profiles or use named executives,
insiders, signers, or donors as personal “wealth leads.”

For the Department of Labor source, it excludes EINs, phone numbers, named
administrators and signers, preparers, broker identities, commissions, and
person-level addresses.

## If a source is unavailable

Open **Market coverage**. Every source reports `LIVE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE`. An outage stays visible; the application does not replace it
with a fake lead.
